Get started

Open the app to see the default Buy vs Rent result for your country and home type.

  • Pick a country and home type to load local market benchmarks
  • On Results, drag the year to inspect the net-worth gap from year 1 to 30
  • Edit price, rate, rent, and other inputs as needed

What you see

  • A verdict: Buy, Rent, or near a tie
  • Ending net-worth gap and the year-by-year advantage curve
  • Monthly payment, holding cost, home value, and remaining loan
  • Each term in the gap formula; PDF export after unlock

Trial and unlock

Viewing the default result is free. The first time you change a parameter, a 24-hour trial begins.

  • After the trial, one purchase unlocks permanent editing
  • PDF export is included in that purchase; it stays locked during the trial
  • After reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen

If something goes wrong

  • Market data did not refresh: check the network; offline, the app uses the bundled table
  • Cannot reach the App Store: check the network and try again
  • Purchase pending: wait for Apple to finish approval
  • Purchase not showing: tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen
