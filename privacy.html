Introduction

Buy or Rent is provided by 北京润智启航科技有限公司. This policy explains how the app handles information. Buy or Rent does not ask you to create an account, and we do not run a user server that stores the inputs you type for a calculation.

In short

Prices, rents, rates, and other values you enter are used on the device to calculate and are not uploaded by the app. When online, the app downloads public market benchmarks to refresh defaults. The trial start time stays in the device Keychain. Purchases are handled by Apple. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Information we do not collect

  • Your name, phone number, email, or an account, unless you email us or submit the support form
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, calendars, or messages
  • Microphone recordings or camera content
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. The app does not show ads
  • The home prices, loan terms, rents, and other assumptions you enter. The app does not send them to our servers

Information handled only on the device

The inputs you enter

Numbers you type or adjust in the app—such as price, down payment, rate, and rent—are used only on the device for the monthly simulation and net-worth comparison. The app does not require a login for that calculation, and it does not upload those values to us.

Trial record

When you first actively change a parameter, the trial start time is stored in the device Keychain so the app can tell whether the 24-hour free trial is still active. Deleting the app removes those records.

Bundled benchmark tables

The app includes public-source property-cost and equity-index tables so defaults still work offline. That is public market data, not your personal information.

Public data downloaded when online

When a network connection is available, the app downloads public market data (property costs and stock-index returns) from GitHub Pages so suggested defaults can be updated. That request does not include the values you typed. If the download fails, the app keeps using the table already on the device.

Information handled by Apple

Unlock is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Payment, receipts, and restore are handled by Apple’s App Store. The app uses StoreKit to know whether the one-time unlock is active on this Apple ID. We do not see your card or your full Apple Account. Please also read Apple’s privacy policy.

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

When you contact us

If you email us or submit the support form, we receive the name, email, subject, and message you provide, and we use them only to reply. The form is received by our host, Netlify, and forwarded to us. Please do not include unnecessary personal details or someone else’s financial information.

How this information is used

  • Run the Buy vs Rent net-worth calculation and charts on your device
  • Remember whether the 24-hour trial has started or ended
  • Ask Apple whether the one-time unlock is valid
  • Refresh public market defaults when online
  • Reply to support requests you send us

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove the trial record and local state stored on the device. Purchase history is kept by Apple and can be reviewed in the App Store. To ask about or delete a message you sent us by email or the form, write to us. People in the EU, UK, California, and similar regions can also request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we actually hold. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

If this handling changes in a meaningful way, we will update this page and the date above. Check this page before you buy or keep using the app.
